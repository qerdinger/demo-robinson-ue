Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver nationwide?

Yes, we deliver across the Philippines. You'll get an SMS or email confirmation once your order has been verified.

Is there a shipping fee?

It depends on your location and how fast you'd like your order. Standard delivery is free above a minimum order amount, with a flat fee below that — and same-day or priority delivery is available in select areas for an extra fee.

How long does delivery take?

Metro Manila orders typically arrive within a few days; provincial orders take a bit longer. Same-day and priority options are available in select areas if you need it sooner.

Do you accept item replacements?

Yes, if an item arrives damaged, expired, or different from what you ordered.

How do I request a replacement?

Reach out to our customer care team within a few days of receiving your order, with the item still in its original, unopened condition.

What payment methods do you accept?

Cash on delivery in select areas, plus debit/credit cards, bank transfer, GCash, and installment plans at checkout.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes, through a partnered installment provider at checkout — you'll be redirected to set up your plan.

Do I need to upload a prescription?

For prescription medicines, yes — you can upload a photo or scan during checkout for our pharmacist to review.

Do you offer Senior Citizen or PWD discounts?

Yes, qualified customers can avail of these discounts by providing a valid ID and prescription where required. The discounted amount is confirmed after we verify your documents.

What is MedSubscribe?

A recurring delivery subscription for the medicines and supplements you take regularly, so you never have to reorder manually.

How do I subscribe to MedSubscribe?

Fill out a short form with your details and any required documents, and our team will follow up to confirm before your first delivery.

How do I cancel my MedSubscribe subscription?

Just email our customer care team a few days before your next scheduled delivery.
